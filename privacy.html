Privacy Policy

PNW Trout · last updated 22 September 2026

PNW Trout collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is nothing to send it to.

What stays on your device

  • The waters you follow. Stored locally on your iPhone. They are never transmitted anywhere and we have no way to see them.
  • Which alerts you have already been shown, so the app does not tell you the same thing twice. Local only.
  • A cached copy of the stocking data, so the app works without a signal at a trailhead.

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

Location

The app asks for your location only when you choose to sort waters by distance or centre the map on yourself. It is used on your device to calculate distances and is never stored, never transmitted, and never seen by us. Accuracy is deliberately requested at kilometre level, which is all that sorting requires. Declining costs you nothing but those two conveniences.

Notifications

Notifications are generated on your device by the app itself. There are no push notifications, so there is no device token and no server holding a list of who to notify.

Network requests

The app downloads three public data files from a static host. It sends no information about you with those requests. As with any request to any website, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) may record standard server log information such as an IP address and timestamp, under their privacy policy. We do not receive, access, or store those logs.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated date above will change with it. Any change that involved collecting data would be described here plainly and in advance.
