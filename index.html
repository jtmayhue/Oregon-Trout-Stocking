Oregon Trout Stocking

Static data endpoint. Rebuilt from ODFW twice weekly.

  • waters.json — stocked waters: coordinates, amenities, species, acreage
  • stockings.json — planned stockings by water
  • status.json — freshness heartbeat
  • quarantine.json — waters held back, with reasons

The schedule is intent, not a receipt. ODFW publishes the week a water is scheduled, not the day, and fish are sometimes diverted. Confirmed stockings come from the zone reports and carry their own date.

Source: Oregon Department of Fish & Wildlife. Every value here traces to a government publication.
