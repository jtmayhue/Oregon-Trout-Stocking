Photo by Cody Jobanek SE Zone – Fishing for trout at Lost Creek Reservoir. Malheur Reservoir – great for Rainbow Trout from 10-22".  Common sizes are 12-16" but we captured fish up to 22" in our sampling this week. We also fly-fished it in the evening and morning and had catches of 10+ trout/hr. We saw people having success trolling and bank fishing with bait as well.  Water temperatures were in the mid 50's. You can launch a boat from the boat ramp. Water is up to the old school bus. If you know you know. Yellowjacket Reservoir – Great fishing without a crowd!  We sampled last week and found fish from 8-18" with a common size of 12-15". We fished leech patterns under an indicator midday and had non-stop action. Water temperatures are in the mid-50's here, too. Lake of the Woods and Fourmile Lake were recently stocked with 12–14-inch rainbow trout. Many streams are closed to protect spawning Redband Trout and Chinook Salmon in the Klamath Basin. Your best bets are Klamath Lake and Klamath River below Keno Dam for Redband Trout. Yellow perch angling still might be good in Lake of the Woods, Gerber Reservoir and Upper Klamath Lake near Rocky Point. Big Brown Trout are cruising the shoreline at Miller Lake. Snow might block access soon. Crappie angling has been excellent at Dog Lake recently. Regulation Updates Please see the regulation update page .
