Photo by Kristin Maxon SW Zone – Rogue-South Coast Wild Steelhead season runs Dec. 1 - Apr. 30. During this season, a Rogue-South Coast Steelhead Validation is required to fish for wild steelhead in the Rogue Basin and South Coast. Anglers who want the opportunity to keep wild winter steelhead must also purchase the Rogue-South Coast Steelhead Harvest Tag. Empire, Saunders, and Butterfield Lakes were stocked recently with legal trout. Bradley Lake and Powers ponds were stocked the week of Nov 15. Diamond Lake is still producing some nice rainbows, along with catch and release brown and tiger trout. The forecast has some cold wet weather for the weekend. Recent rains have kick started fall chinook fishing in the Elk and Sixes Rivers. As always anglers will want to watch the weather and try to fish as river flows are dropping after a rain event. This is a good time of year to visit Elk River Hatchery with fall chinook returning to the hatchery on a daily basis. Rogue Basin Waterbodies AGATE LAKE: bass, carp, crappie, yellow perch, brown bullhead Agate Lake is 14 percent full, and the boat ramp is likely not usable by trailered boats. Bass, panfish, and carp are available. Anglers are encouraged to target carp especially when they are concentrated during the drawn-down portion of the year. Last updated 11/24/26. APPLEGATE RESERVOIR: trout, landlocked spring chinook, largemouth bass, smallmouth bass, black crappie, bluegill Trout fishing should be good especially near the head of the reservoir where reports of very good catches along Carberry Cr have come in recently. Applegate's surface elevation is 1,872 feet, and boat access is limited to the low water boat ramp at French Gulch. The water surface temperature is 50F. Check up-to-date reservoir levels here , or call the Army Corps of Engineers at 800-472-2434 for flow and temperature information. There is a longstanding health advisory for consumption of resident species due to elevated levels of mercury. See Oregon Health Authority consumption guidelines or the sportfishing regulations for more information. Last updated 11/24/26. APPLEGATE RIVER below Applegate Dam: trout The Applegate is very low and clear right now. Try using small presentations and be stealthy approaching the bank to avoid spooking fish. Concentrate on surface chop areas or deeper pools with structure. Only adipose fin clipped rainbow trout may be retained. All wild rainbow and cutthroat trout must be released unharmed. Consult the fishing regulations for more information. Last updated 11/24/26 EMIGRANT LAKE: largemouth and smallmouth bass, black crappie, bluegill, brown bullhead, catfish, rainbow trout Emigrant is currently 15 percent full and launching small boats may still be possible. Anglers can still enjoy fishing from inflatables or other small watercraft, or fishing from shore. Just be prepared for a muddy shoreline. There is a longstanding health advisory for consumption of resident warmwater species in Emigrant for high levels of mercury. Consult the sportfishing regulations or the Oregon Health Authority for more information on consumption guidelines. The most current elevations for Emigrant Reservoir and other Rogue Basin reservoirs can be found on the Bureau of Reclamation's website, here . Last updated 11/24/26. EXPO POND: rainbow trout, largemouth bass, bluegill, black crappie Bass and panfish are available and might be an easy Thanksgiving/Free Fishing Weekend outing. Try rubber baits like senkos or brush hogs on a jig and let them dead drop for bass in cool conditions. Expo Pond is the pond at the Jackson County Fairgrounds located directly adjacent to the access road at Gate 5, at the Southern Oregon RV Park. There are Chinese mystery snails in the most northern pond. They are highly invasive and were illegally dumped here. Please do not move these creatures and drain and dry any personal watercraft before using them in another waterbody. Last updated 11/24/26. FISH LAKE: rainbow trout, brook trout, spring Chinook Fish Lake is slowly filling and is 32 percent full and ice-free. The USFS boat ramp is not available, but the Fish Lake Lodge ramp may still be usable for some boats. Fall fishing can be very good for rainbow trout. Anglers are encouraged to report catches of tiger trout or any other fish stories to Rogue Fish District Staff 541-826-8774. Last updated 11/24/26. HOWARD PRAIRIE: trout, largemouth bass Howard Prairie is 45 percent full and the county ramps are closed for the season. Small boats may be able to launch near the dam. Several anglers have reported high catch rates in recent weeks as the temperatures are cool and trout are widely distributed. Anglers are reminded to remove all vegetation from gear, boots and boats before leaving Howard Prairie. Last updated 11/24/26. HYATT LAKE: trout, largemouth bass Hyatt is currently 33 percent full and recent reports of low water clarity may impact trout fishing. Anglers are reminded to remove all vegetation from gear, boots and boats before leaving Hyatt. Last updated 11/23/26. ILLINOIS RIVER: trout, steelhead NO RECENT REPORTS 11/24/26. LAKE SELMAC (Selmac Lake): trout, largemouth bass, bluegill, crappie Bass and panfish are available, but one recent report said the aquatic vegetation is still a problem. Lake Selmac has its share of non-native aquatic hitchhikers. Boat anglers are asked to do their part to remove as much vegetation as possible before leaving the ramp, as well as drain and dry their vessel before boating in another waterbody. Last updated 11/24/2026. LOST CREEK RESERVOIR: rainbow trout, landlocked spring chinook, bass Lost Creek has dropped to 1,775 ft and is very low. The Takelma boat ramp is open. The Stewart/marina ramp is not available. The surface water temperature readings are unavailable, but likely near 50F.  Trout can be caught at a variety of depths from the surface down to about 50 feet. Lures are often fished behind dodgers, and can be spiked with corn, worms or power worms.  Lost Creek also offers good bank fishing access, and fishing on both sides of the dam can be very effective using PowerBait. Copepods are often present. The fish are safe to eat when cooked, and the copepods can be scraped off. Anglers can get the latest reservoir details by calling the US Army Corps Lost Creek Lake and Applegate Reservoir projects information line at 1-800-472-2434. Last updated 11/24/26. Rogue River, middle: hatchery coho, steelhead, trout Flow in Grants Pass low and clear at 1,200cfs/1.11ft and the forecasted rain may bring the river up over the weekend. If the river comes up pulling plugs or plunking near the heads and tails of pools can be productive for hatchery Coho. Anglers, please use proper catch-and-release techniques , such as keeping fish in the water as much as possible and releasing them quickly. If fish are getting injured with the technique you are using, please change techniques. Remember that smaller rainbow are usually the steelhead that will return in future years. Current flow, temp and turbidity conditions for the Rogue can be found at the USGS stream gauges here , or checking the City of Grants Pass water page's river data here . Last updated 11/24/26. Rogue River, upper: hatchery coho, steelhead, trout Release out of Lost Cr is around 1,000cfs and the flow at Dodge Bridge is holding steady at 1120cfs/2.99ft, around 49F. It's low and clear, but some weather this weekend may cause coho to move upstream. Concentrating on the heads and tails of pools can be productive on the rise or with some rain. As of 11/24/26 the weekly Cole Rivers Hatchery trap counts are; coho 119 new, 412 season, summer steelhead 31 new, 481 season. Spring Chinook have spawned for the year, so please be mindful of where you wade to protect the eggs that are incubating. Rogue River, above Lost Creek Reservoir: trout The Rogue and tributaries above Lost Creek Reservoir remain open year-round, with a daily retention limit of 5 trout (no limit on brook trout). Area snow is patchy, but recent winds may be restricting some access points. Last updated 11/24/26.
