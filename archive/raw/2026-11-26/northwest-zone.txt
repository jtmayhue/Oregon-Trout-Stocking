Summer steelhead on the Siletz. Photo by Justin Robert Jahn NW Zone - Fall Chinook fishing has been slow in the North Coast rivers. The Trask and Wilson Rivers remain the biggest producers but harvest has dropped off even in those basins. Rain predicted over the weekend might bring in a few more fresh Chinook but the season is winding down. In addition beginning Dec 1 all North Coast Rivers and bays close to angling for Chinook. Fishing has really slowed down in the bays with very few fish being harvested. The Trask River and North Fork Nehalem River have hatchery coho salmon runs and a few fish are being caught though, overall, fishing has been slow. Hopefully the rains predicted for Thanksgiving will bring in some fresh fish. Hebo Lake and Town Lake were stocked with trophy trout a couple weeks ago. This was an unscheduled stocking. These are big trout that are fun to catch! Fall Chinook fishing has been slow in the Siletz, Alsea, Siuslaw, Yaquina and Salmon rivers. As a reminder, Chinook are spawning in many systems and anglers are asked to be mindful of fish on redds. Refer to Fall Coastal Salmon Management for more information on season outlines and bag limits. Siltcoos and Tahkenitch lakes are open for wild coho retention. Anglers are having some success in Siltcoos Lake, and more fish should push in with the large tides and expected rain. The Port of Newport public fishing pier will be closed until at least Monday Nov. 30 for safety reasons. Regulation Updates Please see the regulation update page .
