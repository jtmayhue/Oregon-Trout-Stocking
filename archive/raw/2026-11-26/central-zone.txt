Photo by Christian Howarth Central Zone - Following trophy trout stockings, the Fall River has been fishing exceptionally well. Reports of consistent trout fishing on the lower Deschutes around the Trout Creek area. Road closures and snow have limited access to many Central Zone lakes; however, lower elevation waterbodies including Lake Billy Chinook, Haystack Reservoir, and Ochoco Reservoir are good options during the winter months.
