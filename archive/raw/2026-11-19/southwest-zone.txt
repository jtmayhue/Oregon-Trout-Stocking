Photo by Kristin Maxon SW Zone – Reminder to purchase required 2027 validation and harvest tag for winter steelhead in the Rogue Basin or on the South Coast, good through Dec 1 through April 30, 2027. Empire, Saunders, and Butterfield Lakes were stocked recently with legal trout. Bradley Lake and Powers ponds were stocked the week of November 15. Diamond Lake is still producing some nice rainbows, along with catch and release brown and tiger trout. Although the forecast has some cold wet weather for the weekend. Recent rains have kick started fall chinook fishing in the Elk and Sixes Rivers. As always anglers will want to watch the weather and try and fish as rivers flows are dropping after a rain event. This is a good time of year to visit Elk River Hatchery with fall chinook returning to the hatchery on a daily basis. Regulation Updates Please see the regulation update page .
