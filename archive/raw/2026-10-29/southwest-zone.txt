Photo by Kristin Maxon SW Zone – Try some late season trout fishing on either Garrison or Floras Lakes. Garrison Lake in particular usually has some fairly good numbers of trout that carry over from the spring stockings. Anglers are still picking up fall chinook trolling the Chetco River estuary. Empire, Saunders, and Butterfield Lakes were stocked recently with legal trout. Diamond Lake is still producing some nice rainbows, along with catch and release brown trout and tiger trout. Lost Creek Reservoir was stocked with 5,000 legal sized trout last week and reports of anglers being successful has been coming in.
