Snake River channel catfish -Photo by Mike Miles- Weekend's Best Bets Snake Zone – Fall can be one of the best times of the year to fish for smallmouth and crappie as they stock up on forage for the winter. Look for these fish in shallow areas as they move closer to baitfish that are seeking warmer water temperatures near the surface of the reservoirs. Updated changes to salmon and steelhead bag limits adjusted for Snake River started Sept 23. See the regulation updates page for more information.
