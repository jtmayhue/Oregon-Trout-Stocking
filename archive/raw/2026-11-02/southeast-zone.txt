Photo by Cody Jobanek Weekend's Best Bets SE Zone – Fishing for trout at Lost Creek Reservoir. Malheur Reservoir – great for Rainbow Trout from 10-22".  Common sizes are 12-16" but we captured fish up to 22" in our sampling this week. We also fly-fished it in the evening and morning and had catches of 10+ trout/hr. We saw people having success trolling and bank fishing with bait as well.  Water temperatures were in the mid 50's. You can launch a boat from the boat ramp. Water is up to the old school bus. If you know you know. Yellowjacket Reservoir – Great fishing without a crowd!  We sampled last week and found fish from 8-18" with a common size of 12-15". We fished leech patterns under an indicator mid-day and had non-stop action. Water temperatures are in the mid-50's here, too. Yellow perch angling continues to be excellent in numerous locations in the Klamath Basin including Crystal Creek, Fourmile Canal, Sevenmile Canal and Williamson River. Fly and lure fishing for Redband-Rainbow Trout in the Klamath River is good. Many waterbodies will close after October 31 in the Klamath Basin to protect spawning Redband Trout and Chinook Salmon. Now is your last chance to catch and release some monster Redband Trout that have been coming from Upper Klamath Lake this year. Crappie angling has been excellent at Dog Lake recently.
