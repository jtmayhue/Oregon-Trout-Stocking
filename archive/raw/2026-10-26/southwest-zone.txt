Garrison Lake in particular usually has some fairly good numbers of trout that carry over from the spring stockings. Anglers are still picking up fall chinook trolling the Chetco River estuary. Empire, Saunders, and Butterfield Lakes will be stocked by end of week 10/23/26 with legal trout.
