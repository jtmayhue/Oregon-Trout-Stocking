Water temperature is prime right now in the low 50°F. Fishing should remain hot until water temps drop below 45°F. As an added bonus, the low water levels will concentrate fish and may make catching fish easier. Coho fishing has been great on the lower Umatilla River. Expect fishing to remain good into November. Summer steelhead are also present and will become more prominent as the season progresses. Anglers are catching steelhead in both the Grande Ronde and Imnaha Rivers. Focus lower on both rivers this time of year. Kokanee anglers are finding bright fish on the north end of Wallowa Lake. These kokanee will be smaller in size as many larger fish are spawning at the other end of the lake. Kinney Lake was stocked for the winter with trout.
