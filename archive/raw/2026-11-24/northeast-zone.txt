Try your luck before winter sets in. Water temperature is prime right now in the low 50°F. Fishing should remain hot until water temps drop below 45°F. As a bonus, the low water levels will concentrate fish and may make catching fish easier. The Umatilla River should provide good coho fishing opportunity through the end of November. Steelhead are also disbursed throughout the river, but the highest concentrations are still in the lower portion. Anglers are catching steelhead in both the Grande Ronde and Imnaha Rivers. Focus lower on both rivers this time of year. Fishing jigs under bobbers has been effective for catching late fall steelhead. Regulation Updates Please see the regulation update page .
